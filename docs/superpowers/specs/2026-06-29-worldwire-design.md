## 1. Problem & Goal

Fifth sibling of the DeepWire family — after DeepWire (AI/deep-tech), FinWire (finance/markets), DeepWire Celebs (entertainment), and CommWire (commodities) — focused on **all types of news, very broad**. The codebase is cloned from CommWire; UI surfaces, scraping approach, data layer, worker structure, and visual treatment stay identical; only the domain content (sources, category taxonomy, classifier vocabulary, brand identity) changes.

User-stated constraint: *"exactly same news site with the same ui astro and everythings same just the news topic is' World of news for all types of news very broad'... scrape from the best sites."*

## 5. Classifier Rewrite

`src/lib/ai/classify.ts` rewritten with general-news vocabulary:
- `CATEGORY_KEYWORDS`: each of the 7 categories gets ~30–60 distinctive keywords
- `SOURCE_CATEGORY_HINTS`: one entry per source — primary category for that feed, used as fallback when keyword matches are absent
- `categoryMapping`: identity map for the 7 new IDs
- Priority chain in `getPrimaryCategory`: world > us > business > tech > science > sports > culture (geopolitics wins; business beats tech when both match — a "Tesla earnings" story is business, not tech)

## 9. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| NYT/WSJ/FT RSS feeds 403 or paywall | Probe at scaffold; replace with NPR/AP/Bloomberg free feeds |
| Reuters requires user-agent or has rate limits | Probe slowly with `npm run ingest` once; if 429, throttle parser |
| World vs Business overlap (e.g. "global markets") | Source hint overrides keyword match for well-known business wires |
| Tech vs Business overlap (e.g. "Apple earnings") | Priority chain: business > tech when both match |
| Large single-corpus data file | `articles.json` could grow quickly with 25 sources; rely on JSON read API which loads once into memory |
